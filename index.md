# About

Jarrod M. Burges is a mineralogist, petrologist, and machine learning engineer, with expertise in zircon geochemistry and unsupervised machine learning techniques. Burges is a Ph.D. student at Rutgers University [Department of Earth and Planetary Sciences](https://eps.rutgers.edu). He is co-developer of [GeoCORK](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.acags.2026.100364), an advanced research data management tool for U-Pb geochronology data, with in-development expansion into geochemistry. 

Burges builds on his technical and theoretical background in Computer Science and Geology to explore and bridge the gap of applying data-driven techniques by computer scientists to be interpretable and understandable by geologists. 

### Research Interests
* Zircon petrogenesis
* Data-driven approaches to economic ore deposits
* Unsupervised machine learning representations applied to geochemistry
